Kinematic Velocity

$$ \mathbf{v} = \lim_{\Delta t \to 0} \frac{\Delta \mathbf{d}}{\Delta t} = \frac{d\mathbf{d}}{dt} $$

Velocity is fundamentally a vector quantity, meaning it encapsulates both the magnitude (speed) and the precise direction of a body in motion. In classical mechanics, it is the first derivative of position with respect to time.

Universal Gravitation

$$ F = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2} $$

Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation posits that the gravitational force between two masses is directly proportional to the product of their masses and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between their centers.

Newtonian Force

$$ \mathbf{F}_{net} = \frac{d\mathbf{p}}{dt} = m \cdot \mathbf{a} $$

Derived from Newton's Second Law, Force is the fundamental agent of change in the universe. It dictates that the net force applied to a body equals the rate of change of its momentum.

The Lorentz Force

$$ \mathbf{F} = q\mathbf{E} + q(\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B}) $$

Electromagnetism unifies electricity and magnetism into a single fundamental interaction. The Lorentz force law calculates the total force exerted on a charged particle moving through combined electric and magnetic fields.

Photon Energy (Lasers)

$$ E = h \nu = \frac{hc}{\lambda} $$

LASER relies on the quantum nature of light. The Planck-Einstein relation demonstrates that the energy of a photon is directly proportional to its frequency. This concentration of energy allows for high-yield directed-energy initiation.
